# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People seeking a simple, gentle daily skincare ritual and a clear path from choosing products to completing an order.

## Product Purpose

Aureoné is a skincare storefront for discovering products and following a five-step routine. The homepage should make that routine feel simple and easy to follow, then help visitors shop.

## Positioning

The current storefront presents a five-step skincare ritual alongside individual formulas. No unique formula mechanism or independently verified product differentiation has been confirmed; retain existing product facts and avoid inventing efficacy claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a product catalog, product details, offers, and the ritual; manage a shopping bag; and proceed through checkout. The storefront is served as a web application.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve current catalog, product detail, navigation, bag, account, and checkout behavior.
- Keep the five routine stages: Clean, Treat, Glow, Moisturize, Protect.
- Do not change product names, prices, ingredients, instructions, or existing claims as part of a visual redesign.
- Keep the storefront's existing product and editorial content unless a layout requires reorganizing it.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Aureoné.
- Preserve the user-specified palette: Warm Ivory `#F8F5EF`, Champagne Gold `#B99A60`, Soft Black `#211D19`, Light Beige `#EAE1D4`, and Muted Taupe `#A99A89`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing product catalog, product copy, and customer-facing content in `public/assets/js/store.js` and `seed.js`; product information is website content and has not been independently verified.
- Existing brand and product photography under `public/assets/img/`.
- The working storefront and shopping interactions in `public/index.html`, `public/assets/js/store.js`, and `public/assets/css/`.

## Product Principles

- Make the daily routine understandable at a glance.
- Keep product information and shopping actions easy to find.
- Preserve existing commerce behavior.
- Do not fabricate clinical, ingredient, review, or customer claims.
